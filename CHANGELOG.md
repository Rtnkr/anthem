## 1.0

The first release of Anthem, for Mac and Windows.

- **Listening:** spoken references go on screen about a second after they're said; follows *"next verse"*, verse
  ranges and translation switches by voice. On-device English recognition, and Telugu on the Mac.
- **Service plan:** sections, songs, decks, scripture, media, countdowns and stage-timer cues, with thumbnails,
  renaming, drag and drop, looping sections and per-item motion backgrounds.
- **Songs with chords:** ChordPro import, transpose, song order, chords on the stage screen.
- **Scripture:** type-ahead references, an always-visible queue, suggestions, topic search.
- **Screens:** up to four outputs (Audience, Stage, Stream / ATEM with a key colour), themes with picture and video
  backgrounds and a box behind the words, alerts to the screens you choose, Quick Screens.
- **Media:** a Media panel that shows your folders, live backgrounds, pictures and videos with sound.
- **Mac extras:** NDI output, phone remote, linked Macs, sermon notes in Studio, PDF/PowerPoint import, and an AI
  assistant connector.
