# Privacy

Anthem has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not send your sermons, transcripts or settings to
the Author or to anyone else.

**On your computer:** speech recognition, sermon notes and slides are produced on your computer. Songs, services,
transcripts and settings are stored on it:
- Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/Anthem`, with a log in `~/Library/Logs/Anthem.log`.
- Windows: `%APPDATA%\Anthem`.

You can delete them at any time.

**When Anthem uses the network:**
- to download its speech recognition models once (NVIDIA Parakeet, about 450 MB; the optional Telugu model, about
  700 MB, when you choose to download it);
- to check https://github.com/Rtnkr/anthem for updates (GitHub sees an ordinary download request);
- on your local network only, for features you turn on: NDI outputs, linking two Macs, the phone remote and OSC.

**Microphone:** used only while Listen or the microphone test is running.

If you send feedback or attach a log to an issue, that is public on GitHub. Read the log first and remove anything
you would rather not share.
